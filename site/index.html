04
Five verbs. That's the whole tool.
No config. No cloud. One binary that does one thing well.

  

    
render <image>
Any PNG / JPG → ASCII. Five styles: blocks, photo, lofi, braille, sketch.

    
url <site>
Text-aware render of a live webpage. Reads the real DOM, not just pixels.

    
compare a b
Side-by-side diff of two renders. Tune a style until it sings.

    
gallery <in>
One input, every style at once. Pick your look in a glance.

    
to-png <ascii>
Render ASCII back to a crisp PNG — for posts, slides, README headers.

    
doctor
Checks your setup in one shot. Tells you exactly what's missing.
